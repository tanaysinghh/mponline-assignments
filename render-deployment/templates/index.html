Adult Census Income Predictor

Predicts whether a person earns more than $50,000 per year.

About this model

Logistic Regression trained on the UCI Adult Census Income dataset (32,561 records, 14 demographic and employment features), with one-hot encoded categorical variables and standardized numerical variables. Test accuracy is approximately 85%. Predictions are statistical estimates from 1994 census data and should not be used to make decisions about real individuals.
